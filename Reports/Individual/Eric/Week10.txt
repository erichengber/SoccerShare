Demonstration of Applied Knowledge:
This week I applied my knowledge of frontend development, routing, and state management by building a coach onboarding experience in our application. I worked within the existing React and Zustand structure to create a new onboarding page, connect it to the public and protected routes, and make sure coach users were redirected properly after registration and login. I also debugged an issue where newly registered coach accounts were not reaching onboarding as expected, then updated the authentication flow to preserve the correct role and linked user information. This work strengthened the usability of the coach side of the platform and made the onboarding experience more realistic.

Reflection and Lessons Learned:
This week reinforced the importance of testing full user flows rather than only confirming that a page exists. Even though the onboarding page was implemented, the registration-to-onboarding experience still had a gap that only became clear when the full coach signup flow was tested. Fixing that issue showed how important it is for authentication state, routing logic, and role-based navigation to work together correctly. Overall, this experience highlighted the value of end-to-end thinking and careful debugging when building user-facing features.
